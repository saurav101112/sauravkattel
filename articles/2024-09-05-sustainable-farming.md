Exploring modern irrigation methods and soil conservation to improve crop yield in the hilly regions of Nepal — a practical look at sustainable practices.

## Key techniques

- **Drip irrigation** to cut water use by up to 60%
- **Terrace management** to reduce soil erosion on slopes
- **Cover cropping** with legumes to fix nitrogen naturally
- **Rainwater harvesting** with farm ponds (Pokhari)
